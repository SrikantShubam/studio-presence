The Joinery Philosophy · Non-Negotiable Standard

“Why We Say No to Remote Sites: Bespoke Joinery Demands the Supervisor’s Boots on Site Every Single Morning.”

In residential interior architecture, millimeter tolerances matter. Bihar's extreme seasons—intense monsoon humidity followed by bone-dry winter winds—cause unseasoned structures to expand and contract. When a mason's brick wall is out of plumb by 4mm, waiting 48 hours for an outstation supervisor compromises the entire modular carcass.

Because our studio is in Boring Road and our workshop is at Digha, our supervisors drive to every active site by 08:30 AM. By capping our operating radius at 150 km (a 2.5-hour morning drive), we guarantee that Ashish Kumar and his lead supervisors personally touch your woodwork every week.
