The Friday photo update protocol

Every active client receives a structured 12-photo WhatsApp report every Friday at 17:00 from their assigned site supervisor. You will never have to chase contractors or wonder what happened on site.

  • 01
    Core joinery verification: Photos of BWR plywood stamps and 710-grade core before laminates are pressed.
  • 02
    Carcass plumb & level audit: Spirit level placed on kitchen base cabinets and wardrobe carcasses to verify zero tilt.
  • 03
    Debris clearance pledge: Confirmation photo showing site swept clean, sawdust bagged, and raw materials secured for the weekend.
