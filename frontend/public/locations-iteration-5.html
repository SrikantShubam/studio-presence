Step-by-Step Studio Protocol

What Happens When You Bring Your Floor Plans

01

Plan Unrolling & Dimension Audit

Your builder floor plan or architect's CAD drawing is laid flat across the 12-ft teak table. We compare your sanctioned drawings against true carpet area, identifying dead corners, electrical shafts, and plumbing drops.

02

Patna Masonry Reality Check

Patna apartment walls are rarely true 90-degree plumbs. We cross-reference typical beam offsets and monsoon ground seepage risks in your specific locality (Boring Road, Patliputra, or Kankarbagh) before drafting fixed joinery.

03

Side-by-Side Material Pairing

Instead of small 2-inch catalog squares, you pull full-scale veneer leaves and 1.0mm laminates from Wall Racks A & B directly onto your drawing. You examine wood grain under true daylight from the East window bay.

04

Live Millimeter Carpentry Drafting

Sit directly beside Ashish Kumar at Desk 01 as your storage heights, internal wardrobe partitions, and kitchen drawer depths are drafted live in AutoCAD. You walk out with an exact millimeter BOQ, not a vague lump-sum guess.
