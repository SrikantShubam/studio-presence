बैठक का अनुभव

What happens across
the drawing table.

When you visit our Boring Road studio, you sit directly with Ashish Kumar and our senior interior architect. Here is what we do in forty-five minutes:

01

Redlining the Builder's Plan

We take a red pencil to your flat's layout. We identify door conflicts, dead wardrobe corners, and ventilation traps builder marketing renders conceal.

02

Touching Genuine Materials

You hold real 710 marine plywood core samples, scratch test laminate coatings, and compare European soft-close drawer slides with your own hands.

03

Honest Square-Foot Arithmetic

You leave with an unpadded, room-by-room budget bracket. No pressure, no aggressive sales follow-up calls. The decision remains yours.

The direct founder commitment

Direct phone line to the studio bench.

No call-center agents. You talk directly with the people who supervise the carpenters, detail the drawings, and hand over the keys.
