Supervisor dispatch protocol

Three things verified
at your Patna property.

When our supervisor arrives at your flat or house, we do not conduct a sales meeting. We audit the physical reality of the structure so joinery fits with zero gaps.

01. Plaster Plumb & Beam Clearances

We check wall squaring and ceiling level variations. False ceilings and wardrobe shutters fail when built to nominal brochure dimensions rather than actual site geometry.

02. Moisture & Wall Humidity Audit

Bihar monsoons cause damp seepage. We probe wall dampness behind future kitchen sinks and wardrobe backs to prescribe 710 BWP plywood where ordinary commercial ply delaminates.

03. Utility Conduits & Plumbing Points

We verify switchboard lines, chimney shafts, and drain pipe slopes before cabinet boxes are fabricated in our workshop.
