Ritual 01 First 15 minutes

Unrolling your blueprint or rough sketch

We lay your floor plan on our solid teak consultation table. Whether it is a builder brochure, an AutoCAD layout, or measurements written on a notebook, we map out room dimensions, natural light angles, and ventilation flows.

Ritual 02 Next 30 minutes

Candid carpentry & material inspection

We guide you to our material racks. You inspect the cross-section of genuine BWR 710 marine plywood versus commercial particle boards. We test Hafele soft-close hinges on full-scale carcases so you understand joint tolerances before spending a single rupee.

Ritual 03 Final 15 minutes

Transparent line-item budget over hot chai

While you enjoy fresh cardamom ginger chai brewed in-house, Ashish computes a realistic line-item budget. Raw plywood sheets, laminate brand rates, hardware units, and skilled carpentry labor are itemized clearly without lumped square-foot traps.
