The Material Archive & Sample Touch Room

In digital interior catalogs, cheap commercial ply looks identical to calibrated IS:710 Marine board. But in Patna’s extreme monsoon climate — where relative humidity surges past 85% between July and September — poor adhesives swell, thin laminates fracture, and zinc hinges sag within two seasons. Our Boring Road studio exists so you can scratch, weigh, and verify real materials before paying advances.

BENCHMARK 01 · CORE SUBSTRATE

Boiling Water Proofing (IS:710)

IS:710 BWP Marine 72 Hours Boiling Water Test. 100% Phenol Formaldehyde Synthetic Resin bonding. Zero delamination.
Commercial MR Grade Fails within 2 hours of boiling water. Urea Formaldehyde bond dissolves; layers separate and rot.
Every wardrobe carcass and kitchen box at Ashish Interiors uses calibrated IS:710 plywood.
BENCHMARK 02 · SURFACE ARMOR

1.0mm Merino vs 0.8mm Liner

1.0mm Merino HPL Solid phenolic backing. 4H scratch resistance, zero edge chipping when cut with diamond blades.
0.8mm Market Liner Brittle paper backing. Shows ugly white hairline fractures at shutter edges during monsoon expansion.
We keep cut test strips of both on our sample table so you can inspect edge bevels with your fingernails.
BENCHMARK 03 · OPERATING HARDWARE

Häfele Metalla 300 SS vs Zinc

Häfele Metalla 300 AISI 304 Stainless Steel. 200,000 cycle endurance, 96-hour salt spray anti-rust certification.
Brass-Plated Zinc Die-cast zinc core with thin flash plating. Corrodes green and sags under 18mm shutter weight in 18 months.
All kitchen shutters are mounted on functioning Häfele demo rigs right inside the studio.

Studio Transparency Guarantee

The In-Studio Scratch & Water Test

When you arrive at 2nd Floor Shivam Complex, we hand you a stainless steel carpenter’s awl and a water dropper. We invite you to perform two non-negotiable stress tests right in front of us before taking an estimate home:

  • 01
    The 1.0mm Coin Scratch Test: Drag a coin with full body weight across our 1.0mm Merino laminate sample. Observe zero surface scuffing or white backing emergence.
  • 02
    The End-Grain Water Droplet Test: Drop water directly onto the raw cut core of our IS:710 Marine block. Notice how the water beads without soaking into the phenolic adhesive.
