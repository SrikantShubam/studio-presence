Frequently asked location queries

Where can I meet the designer to review floor plans?

You can meet Ashish Kumar and lead designers at our Boring Road Atelier (Shivam Complex), Bailey Road Studio (near Saguna More), or Kankarbagh desk.

Can clients inspect joinery cutting before installation?

Yes. Our Digha Ghat workshop is open for client visits by appointment on weekdays so you can inspect IS:710 marine ply and edge banding in person.

Do you take home interior projects in Hajipur?

Yes. We maintain a dedicated regional site office at Station Road, Ramashis Chowk in Hajipur with resident supervisors managing turnkey bungalow and duplex interiors.
