THIS
STUDIO

We took the second floor above the crossing in 2016, three years after starting in a single rented room two lanes away. It is the only office we have.

The floor is a drawing office, a sample room and one long table. There is no reception, because nine times out of ten the person who greets you is the person who will draw your flat. Laminates, ply cross-sections, granite offcuts and hardware samples are all on open racks — clients handle the actual material rather than approving a swatch on a screen.

The work this studio takes is Patna and about 150 km around it: Hajipur, Danapur, Bihta, Muzaffarpur. Roughly seven in ten projects are flats between 900 and 1,500 sq ft; the rest are small professional offices, guest houses and the occasional shop. We do not bid outside that radius, because the whole model depends on a supervisor being able to reach a site the same morning.

The workshop is a separate floor five minutes away, which moved and doubled in 2025. Clients are welcome to see their joinery being cut — ask on a weekday and Manoj will take you across.
