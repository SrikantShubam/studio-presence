WHAT WE DO IN PATNA HOMES

MODULAR
KITCHENS

FROM ₹1,450 PER SQ FT OF KITCHEN AREA A 60 sq ft Patna kitchen typically lands between ₹1.9 and ₹2.6 lakh, hob and chimney excluded.

A modular kitchen is really a set of decisions about how your family cooks, not a catalogue of shutters. We start there.

In most Patna flats the kitchen is between 45 and 80 sq ft, with one window, a fixed water line and a gas point the builder put where it suited him. The first thing we work out is the triangle — sink, hob, fridge — and whether the wall between kitchen and dining can come down. About half our clients say yes, and it is usually the single change they mention a year later.

Then come the choices you will actually have to make: carcass in BWP marine ply or the cheaper BWR (we build in BWP under the sink regardless), shutters in laminate, acrylic or membrane, and a counter in granite or quartz. Granite is still the sensible answer for most Patna kitchens — it takes the heat of a pressure cooker, it survives turmeric, and a local slab costs a third of imported quartz. We push people towards tall units over lofts, because nobody over fifty wants to climb for the atta dabba.

Fabrication takes 18 to 25 days in our workshop, installation another three, and we do not start until the plumbing and the electrical points are set — moving a chimney point after the counter is down means cutting granite, and cut granite never looks the same again.
