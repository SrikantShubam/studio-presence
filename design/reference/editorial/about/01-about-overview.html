How we work

Every project begins with a room-by-room survey, family routine notes and a budget discussion that separates must-have work from optional upgrades.

The drawings stay useful on site: cabinet depths, switch positions, appliance clearances and storage heights are checked before workshop fabrication begins.

Clients get material shortlists, procurement dates and handover notes so the home can be maintained without depending on memory or verbal promises.
