01

Measured drawings

Final dimensions are marked after plaster, tile and utility points are visible, so fabrication does not depend on assumed room sizes.

02

Material lock

Ply grade, laminate code, edge band, hardware level and counter selection are frozen before purchase orders are issued.

03

Workshop build

Cabinets are cut, pressed, edged and checked for appliance clearances before dispatch to the site.

04

Installation sequence

Base units, wall units, counters, shutters, lights and handles are fitted in order to avoid rework and avoid damage to finished surfaces.
