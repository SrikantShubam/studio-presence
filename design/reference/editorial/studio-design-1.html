ABOUT
THE STUDIO

Ashish Interiors began in 2013 with one carpenter, one measuring tape and a rented room off Boring Road. The work has always been ordinary homes done properly — a family's first flat, a mother's kitchen, an office for four people.

We still keep the crew in-house and still visit every site ourselves. Nothing leaves the workshop that we would not put in our own house.
