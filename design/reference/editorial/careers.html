TWENTY PEOPLE, FIVE LIVE SITES, ONE WORKSHOP

WORK
WITH US

We are twenty people running four to six live sites at a time out of one studio and one workshop off Boring Road. If you join the drawing office you will be measuring flats yourself, drawing joinery sections that our own carpenters build from, and standing in the room while they go in.

A typical project is a 1,000–1,500 sq ft flat on an eight to eleven week programme, budget between six and fourteen lakh, for a family who is spending most of what they have. There is no rendering department and no marketing team to hand a scheme to. What you draw is what gets built, and you will find out within the month whether it worked.

WHAT WE
LOOK FOR

01

YOU CAN DRAW A SECTION

Not a render — a section. A wardrobe at 1:10 with the carcass thickness, the shutter overlay and the hinge position on it, that a carpenter can build from without calling you. If your portfolio is all elevations and moodboards we will ask you to draw one in the interview.

02

YOU ARE COMFORTABLE ON SITE

Half this job is standing in dust with a tape measure, arguing pleasantly with a plumber about a waste position. If site work is something you hope to grow out of, this is the wrong studio.

03

YOU CAN TALK ABOUT MONEY

Our clients are spending their savings and they ask direct questions about cost. You will need to say “that detail adds forty thousand, here is what I would do instead” without flinching or getting defensive.

04

YOU WRITE THINGS DOWN

Every decision on our sites is recorded — a dated schedule, weekly photographs, a punch list. People who work from memory struggle here, and people who keep notes find the job much easier than it looks.

HOW TO
APPLY

SEND IT TO studio@ashishinteriors.in Subject line: the role name. No cover letter needed.

WHAT TO SEND

  • A portfolio PDF, under 15 MB. Six to ten pieces of work is plenty — we would rather see four projects properly documented than twenty thumbnails.
  • Include at least one working drawing if you have one. A dimensioned section or a shop drawing tells us more than any render.
  • A CV, one page. Where you studied, where you have worked, what you built there.
  • Two or three lines in the email about why this studio rather than a bigger one. That is the whole cover letter.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

Ashish or Sunita reads every application themselves, usually within five working days, and you will get a reply either way — including a no. If we want to meet you it is one conversation at the studio, an hour, with your drawings on the table. For design roles there is a short paid exercise: draw one wardrobe section, at home, in your own time.

We do not do panel interviews, personality tests or unpaid trial weeks.
