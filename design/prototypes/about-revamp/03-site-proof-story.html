Site proof story

The project should feel clear before it feels beautiful

This About concept turns the studio story into a client journey: what happens after an enquiry, when cost is discussed, when drawings freeze, and how the homeowner knows work is moving.

The path

Six moments where trust is won

01

Enquiry

The visitor shares location, flat size, rooms, possession date and a rough budget range.

02

Site visit

The studio measures the space and checks utility points before promising scope or cost.

03

Budget conversation

Must-have work is separated from optional upgrades so the client can choose with control.

04

Design freeze

Layouts, finish boards, storage dimensions and major decisions are signed before execution.

05

Site supervision

Progress is coordinated against milestones, with photos and notes when decisions change.

06

Handover

Snags, bills, material references and care notes are closed before the project is called done.
