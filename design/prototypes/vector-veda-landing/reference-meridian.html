Frequently
asked questions.

The useful answers before you choose a plan.

What is Studio Presence?+

A website product and setup service from Vector Veda for interior design studios. It brings your portfolio, services, studio story, and contact routes into one place.

What do you need from us?+

Your logo, studio details, selected photographs, service information, and any approved testimonials or project copy you want to publish.

Can we see it before it goes live?+

Yes. The process includes a review before launch. A preview does not automatically become your public website.

How much does it cost?+

Presence is ₹15,000, Presence Plus is ₹25,000, and Growth is ₹45,000–55,000. Your quote confirms the final scope.

Does it guarantee enquiries?+

No. It gives people a clearer way to understand and contact your studio. Rankings and enquiries depend on many factors.
