Before you decide

A few fair
questions.

What's included, what you need, and what happens after the website goes live.

Have something else in mind?
What exactly is Studio Presence?+−

A website product and setup service from Vector Veda for interior design studios. You provide the studio information and project material; we turn it into a website with a portfolio, service information and clear contact routes. It is not an interior design service or a lead marketplace.

We already use Instagram. Why have a website?+−

Keep using Instagram to share your work. Your website gives a referred or interested client one organised place to understand your projects, services, process and areas served. It is a link you can share wherever someone discovers your studio.

Is the website going to look like another studio’s?+−

Your website uses your brand, photographs, services and story. The layout is adapted within the agreed design system. A completely new visual identity or a major structural change is discussed separately so the scope stays clear.

What do you need from us?+−

Your studio name and logo, contact details, service areas, service descriptions and selected project photographs. For richer plans, project descriptions, before-and-after images, reviews and team information make the extra sections useful. Only share material you have permission to publish.

Can I see it before it goes live?+−

Yes. The process includes reviewing the website before launch. We confirm the content, agreed changes and live setup together. A preview does not automatically become your public website.

Can our team update the website?+−

Client access and content editing depend on the plan and the access agreed for your team. We explain the available controls during handover. Design changes, new page types and changes outside your plan are scoped separately.

Are these monthly prices? What about renewals?+−

The prices shown are for website setup, not a monthly subscription price. Your quote sets out domain, hosting, renewals, applicable taxes and any ongoing support. We agree those terms before work starts.

Can we start small and upgrade later?+−

Yes. Start with the plan that matches your current content. We can scope additional portfolio depth, enquiry tools, team pages or other Growth features later. The upgrade price depends on the extra work required.

Will the website guarantee Google rankings or new clients?+−

No. A useful website helps people understand and contact your studio, but rankings and enquiries depend on many factors. Page metadata and search foundations are different from an ongoing SEO or advertising campaign.

Can we use our own domain and regional language content?+−

An existing domain can be discussed during setup. Language options are scoped with Growth or as an agreed addition. We confirm content, translations and domain arrangements before launch.
