FAQs

Questions before you start.

How many projects should I share?+

Start with the work that best explains your studio. The plan determines the depth of the presentation, not a requirement to fill every slot.

What kind of design work is included?+

The website, its page structure, responsive layout, and agreed content presentation. A new brand identity or major structural change is scoped separately.

How fast will the website be ready?+

Timing depends on the material, review cycle, and final scope. The delivery route is agreed before the work begins.

Can we add more later?+

Yes. Additional project depth, pages, team content, and other requirements can be scoped when the material is ready.
