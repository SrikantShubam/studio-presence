## 2. What we need from you

Within **7 days** of this agreement, you will provide: business details, contact numbers, service
descriptions, project photographs, testimonials, and any logo or brand material you have.

If content is not provided within 7 days, the project pauses. When you return, the timeline restarts
from that point. We will not hold an unpaid slot indefinitely, and we will not chase content
repeatedly.

You confirm that all photographs, text and materials you send us are yours to use, or that you have
permission to use them. If a third party claims otherwise, that's on you, not us.

---

## 3. What's included and what isn't

**Included:** adding new content blocks within existing pages — for example an "Awards" section on
your About page, or a new project entry in your portfolio.

**Requires Presence Plus pricing:** adding an entirely new page — for example a standalone "Awards"
page or a new "Team" page — or restructuring the site navigation.

**Always quoted separately:** changes to layout, visual design, or overall look; any feature not
listed in Schedule A; ongoing SEO, advertising, or social media services.

You get **one consolidated round of revisions** at the staging review stage (§4). Further rounds are
quoted separately.

## 4. Payment

| Stage | Amount | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Advance | 50% | On signing, before work begins |
| Balance | 50% | On staging approval, before the site goes live |

**How this works in practice:**
- No work begins until the advance has cleared our bank.
- We deliver the finished site on a preview link on our own domain for your review.
- Once you approve it, the balance falls due.
- **The site is moved to your domain only after the balance clears.** This is not negotiable and
  applies to every client without exception.

The preview link stays active for 14 days after approval. After that a late fee of 2% per week
applies to the outstanding balance.

All prices are exclusive of GST, charged at 18% as applicable.

---

## 5. Who owns what

**You own your content.** All text, photographs, project details, testimonials and brand materials
you provide remain entirely yours.

**We own the code.** The website template, component library, and underlying code are our
pre-existing intellectual property and remain ours. What you receive is a licence to use the
configured website built for you, for your business, for as long as you like.

This is standard practice for template-based work, and it's why the price is what it is — you're
sharing the cost of something built once and refined across many projects rather than paying for
bespoke development.

*(Note: under the Indian Copyright Act the creator owns copyright unless it is expressly assigned.
This clause makes the position explicit in both directions rather than leaving it to default.)*

## 10. Confidentiality and your data

We will not share your business information, content, or analytics with anyone else. Because our
clients are often direct competitors in the same city, we maintain an internal policy of never
comparing or sharing data between clients, even anonymised.

Enquiry form submissions are retained for 12 months and then deleted.

---

## 11. If either of us wants to end this

**Before the site goes live:** if you cancel after the advance has been paid, the advance is not
refundable — work has been done. If we cannot deliver, we refund the advance in full.

**After the site is live:** you may stop working with us at any time. On request, and for a flat
handover fee of ₹`____` (₹5,000–8,000), we will provide a complete export of your website code and
content.

The handover package includes code and content. It does not include our accounts, credentials, or
backend access — those remain ours.

Your domain is yours throughout and is unaffected.

---

## 12. Limits

We build websites. We do not guarantee search rankings, traffic volumes, or a number of enquiries —
nobody honestly can, and anyone who does is overselling.

Our total liability under this agreement is limited to the amount you have paid us.

The clauses on intellectual property (§5), confidentiality (§10), and limits (§12) survive the end
of this agreement.
