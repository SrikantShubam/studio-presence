# Studio Presence market pricing and offer research

Date: 2026-09-18  
Research scope: Indian interior-design studios  
Price basis: public INR prices visible on provider pages on 2026-09-18, unless noted otherwise

## Bottom line

Keep the three prices. The public sample supports them when each tier is sold as a different
business job rather than as a larger pile of pages.

- **Presence, ₹15,000:** make a studio easy to check and contact. Position it as a fixed-scope,
  interior-specific multi-page site, not as the cheapest website in India.
- **Presence Plus, ₹25,000:** help a prospect understand the studio's work and qualify the enquiry.
  Make project proof, before/after material, reviews, and enquiry qualification the reason to move up.
- **Growth, ₹45,000–55,000:** give a larger practice a structured web presence and lead workflow.
  Quote this range against a named page list and named additions. Do not describe it as an unlimited
  bespoke build.

The sample is not a market average. It is a set of public offers used to calibrate positioning.
The offers differ in page count, design depth, ownership, support, hosting, tax treatment, and
whether the buyer is doing the work themselves. A lower headline price is not automatically a
lower total cost or a like-for-like substitute.

## Internal product context

The repository's product documents describe the following ladder:

- **Presence:** the working studio site, with portfolio, services, about, process, testimonials,
  Instagram, FAQ, contact/map, and project detail capability.
- **Presence Plus:** Presence with deeper project proof and selected service or area pages where the
  studio can provide genuinely distinct content.
- **Growth:** the larger-practice package, with a named set of practice, editorial, project,
  location, lead, and analytics capabilities. The internal page inventory describes 27 possible
  layouts at full scope, but the quote must name which pages are actually included.

These are internal product facts, not claims about the market. The relevant internal sources are
`docs/product/page-inventory.md`, `docs/product/feature-tiers.md`,
`docs/finance/pricing-and-negotiation.md`, `docs/legal/services-agreement.md`, and
`docs/sales/interiors-page-copy.md`.

## Recommended positioning by tier

### Presence, ₹15,000

**Business job:** get into the consideration set.

Lead with the result the owner can verify: a prospect can see the studio's work, services, location,
and contact path in one owned destination. Make the fixed scope visible. Name the included page types,
one review round, content requirements, delivery dependency, and the domain/hosting treatment.

The comparison point is not “a website for ₹15,000.” It is a specialist site that is ready to send
after an Instagram post, WhatsApp conversation, referral, or Google search. Public specialist offers
at ₹15,000 and generic offers at ₹14,999 show that the price is credible, but they do not prove a
conversion or search outcome. [S1] [S4]

Recommended role in the ladder: the clear first step for a studio with good work but no dependable
place to present it.

### Presence Plus, ₹25,000

**Business job:** reduce uncertainty before the first call.

Use the additional price to buy better explanation and qualification, not merely more navigation.
The public generic comparison is especially close here: LaunchRoom lists ₹24,999 for a five-page
brochure site with lead capture, WhatsApp, and basic SEO. [S4]

Presence Plus should therefore make its extra value concrete through the studio's own material:
project stories, before/after evidence where the client has permission to publish it, review content,
an enquiry form with useful qualifiers, and service or area pages only when each page has distinct,
real content. Do not promise that these elements produce a given number of leads.

Recommended role in the ladder: the choice for a studio that has enough work and proof to explain
why a prospect should enquire now.

### Growth, ₹45,000–55,000

**Business job:** support a larger practice without starting from zero.

The public sample gives this range a credible reference point. LaunchRoom's Scale package starts at
₹44,999, while WebsiteIn5000 lists a ₹55,000 interior-designer base plan and separately prices CRM,
admin, and extra-page additions. [S3] [S4]

Keep Growth as a scoped extension of the existing foundation. Quote the lower end for the standard
Growth page set. Use the upper end only when the quote names the extra practice, editorial, location,
lead, analytics, or integration work that justifies it. Any genuinely new integration or client
portal should be a separate line item or change order.

Recommended role in the ladder: the choice for a studio with a body of work, more than one service or
location, a team or press story to present, and someone who will use the enquiry data.

## Justdial: useful channel, unreliable single-number comparison

Justdial does publish a public advertising page. At the time of access it displayed a Standard plan
at ₹132/day and “onwards” discounted figures of ₹112/day for one year, ₹56/day for two years, and
₹38/day for three years. The same page says the final price is calculated from the selected category
and city and is shown at checkout. [S8]

That is not a reliable universal advertising price for an Indian interior-design studio. It is an
indicative price card whose final amount depends on inputs that have not been fixed here. Justdial's
own terms also describe package and cost-per-enquiry arrangements, and its investor materials say
pricing depends on category, geography, customer budget, and package type. [S9] [S10]

Do not convert the displayed “onwards” number into a firm annual comparison, claim that Justdial
costs a fixed amount for interior studios, or say that a website replaces directory advertising.
The defensible copy is narrower: a directory listing is a paid discovery channel with recurring,
category- and city-dependent pricing; Studio Presence is an owned website and enquiry destination
with a one-time build price and separately stated operating costs.

## One-time setup, hosting, and domain renewals

Public offers show several valid commercial patterns:

- A one-time build can include setup and a limited support period. LaunchRoom explicitly calls its
  build a one-time investment and includes domain and hosting setup in Basic. [S4]
- A provider can include the first year and separate renewal. Sapient says first-year hosting and a
  .com domain are included. [S5]
- A provider can separate hosting, maintenance, and domain from the build. Pointers shows ₹499/month
  hosting and maintenance plus a separate ₹899–1,499/year domain range, and explains that usage can
  change the hosting price. [S6]
- A specialist can exclude domain and hosting from the development price. WebsiteIn5000 says those
  charges are not included. [S3]

Studio Presence should choose one policy and write it consistently in the sales page, quote, and
agreement:

1. State whether the displayed tier price is one-time build only or includes first-year domain and
   hosting.
2. State who owns the domain account. The safe promise is “the domain is registered in your name”
   only if the operating process actually does that.
3. State the renewal amount or a clear formula, who pays it, what hosting and support cover, and what
   happens if the client does not renew.
4. State whether email, paid APIs, CRM, stock media, copywriting, photography, and future structural
   work are included.

The internal documents are not yet fully aligned on this point. `docs/sales/interiors-page-copy.md`
states that all plans include domain and hosting for year one with a ₹1,500–2,500 renewal, while
`docs/legal/services-agreement.md` leaves the setup and annual amounts blank and allows either
client-supplied or provider-arranged hosting. The public offer should wait for one approved policy.

## GST and “registered firm” claims

Use factual, narrow language. A written contract, an incorporated company, a partnership-firm
registration, and GST registration are different claims.

CBIC says a person whose aggregate turnover exceeds the applicable threshold, or who falls under a
compulsory-registration condition, must register. CBIC also says an unregistered person cannot issue
a GST tax invoice, while a registered invoice carries the prescribed GSTIN and tax details. [S11]
[S12]

Until Studio Presence has verified its own legal entity and active GST status, do not publish:

- “GST registered”;
- a GSTIN;
- “proper GST invoice”;
- “registered firm” as a vague trust signal; or
- a tax-exclusive or tax-inclusive claim that the actual invoice process cannot support.

Once verified, name the exact legal entity and use the applicable registration details. If the firm is
not GST-registered, say only what is true about the price and invoice, and do not add GST to a quote
without the required basis. This is a compliance boundary, not a positioning flourish.

## What can safely be said about ROI

The website can make a credible economic case without promising a return. A prospect may use it to
check a studio after seeing it on Instagram, receiving a referral, finding it on Google, or comparing
several studios. That is a reason to own the destination. It is not evidence that the site will
generate a fixed number of enquiries, projects, or rupees.

Google's own tools separate visibility and response metrics. Search Console reports impressions,
clicks, click-through rate, and average position. Google Analytics can measure conversions based on
events and compare paid, organic, and direct traffic. [S13] [S14]

The safe offer is therefore measurement setup and a review habit, not an ROI guarantee. Track the
enquiry path where the selected tier supports it, ask new enquiries how they found the studio, record
qualified enquiries and proposals, and compare the website-assisted baseline after launch. Avoid
“pays for itself,” “three clients guaranteed,” “5× return,” “more leads guaranteed,” and claims that a
website alone creates rankings.

## Recommended copy implications

- **Hero:** “A fixed-scope website for interior studios. Make your work easy to check, understand,
  and enquire about.” Keep the price visible at ₹15,000 and offer a preview only if the preview
  process is real and resourced.
- **Presence:** “Your studio's owned starting point.” List the exact page and contact scope.
- **Presence Plus:** “More proof before the first call.” Show the project, review, and qualification
  additions that are actually included.
- **Growth:** “A structured web presence for a growing practice.” Show the named page set and quote
  the upper end only against named additions.
- **Trust block:** Promise a written scope, a real review process, client-owned domain handling, and
  the actual tax treatment. Do not borrow GST, registered-firm, testimonial, client-logo, or
  performance claims that have not been verified.
- **Comparison line:** “Directory listings and social channels can still bring discovery. This is
  the website you control after someone wants to check the studio.” Do not use an invented Justdial
  annual saving or a claim that the website replaces every paid channel.

## Decision update — 2026-09-19

The dated market observations above remain unchanged. The current Studio Presence offer has since
been revised:

- Presence is ₹17,999 including GST.
- Presence Plus is ₹29,999 including GST.
- Growth starts at ₹53,999 including GST and is custom work outside the approved templates.
- Growth Custom starts at ₹64,999 including GST when the project needs deeper custom design,
  migration, custom editor work, or five revision rounds.
- All packages include one month of bounded launch support.
- Presence Plus includes the journal with a 100-post limit. Presence can enable it for ₹3,999.
- Growth can include up to 1,000 published posts when the custom scope requires it.

These decisions supersede the older proposed prices and tier descriptions in the body of this dated
research note. The competitor prices in the research tables are historical observations, not Studio
Presence prices.
