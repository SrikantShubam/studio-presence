## GST and payment handling

The firm is GST-registered. GST collected from the customer is not profit.

For each sale, record:

1. customer collection including GST;
2. service value before GST;
3. output GST;
4. payment fee and GST on that fee;
5. intern commission on the service value before GST;
6. per-sale tooling and operating cost; and
7. the remaining amount before shared overhead and income tax.

Use the actual payment provider settlement statement. The current working payment assumption is 0.4%
plus GST on the provider's fee base.

## Negotiation rules

Do not discount the price without reducing scope or receiving a clear commercial trade.

Acceptable trades include:

- 100% upfront payment;
- fewer initial projects;
- fewer locations;
- fewer revision rounds;
- no custom branding or icon work; or
- case-study permission.

Do not discount Growth custom work while leaving the custom scope unchanged. Reduce the scope or
keep the quoted price.

## Customer-facing price language

Use:

> Presence starts at ₹17,999 including GST. Presence Plus is ₹29,999 including GST. Growth projects
> start at ₹53,999 including GST and are quoted against the custom design and implementation scope.

Do not claim leads, rankings, revenue, WhatsApp conversations, or a guaranteed return.
