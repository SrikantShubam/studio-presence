# Unit 4 — Bold Modern — Home page

Attach: none — no direct reference exists for this identity, expect more iteration than the other units

Paste `00-identity-system.md` first, then this:

```
Design the complete HOME PAGE for Ashish Interiors in this identity, desktop, top to bottom, same
section list as Unit 1, expressed in the Bold Modern visual language — alternating black and white
full-bleed section blocks, bold filled numerals, red-orange accent used confidently:

1. NAV — per this identity's Global Chrome variant, on the opening colour block.
2. HERO — full-bleed black block, huge white/red-orange headline, high-contrast or duotone photo,
   red-orange CTA "Book a free consultation".
3. QUICK-ACTIONS ROW — WhatsApp · Call · Directions · Instagram, bold bordered buttons.
4. TRUST ELEMENT — white block, bold filled numerals for the key stats.
5. SERVICES — black block, bold filled numerals 01/02/03 (solid, not outlined), stark white type.
6. FEATURED PROJECTS — white block, duotone photo treatment, diagonal cut dividers between tiles.
7. ABOUT — alternating block, bold statement headline, one strong photo.
8. PROCESS — bold filled numerals in a diagonal or angular timeline, not a soft curve.
9. TESTIMONIALS — high-contrast card treatment, red-orange accent on the featured quote.
10. INSTAGRAM STRIP — duotone-treated tiles to match the rest of the page.
11. FAQ — bold caps heading, thick dividers (not hairlines), "+" icons in red-orange.
12. CONTACT — black block, red-orange CTA repeated.
13. FOOTER — per this identity's Global Chrome variant, full black.

REQUIRED: at least 2 signature devices from this identity's list.

ANTI-GOALS: no ghost/outline numerals (Editorial), no soft rounded corners or organic shapes (Warm
Contemporary), no muted taupe-gold restraint (Premium) — this identity should look distinctly
different from the other three. Otherwise: no SaaS centred-hero pattern, no purple/blue/teal
replacing the red-orange accent, no glassmorphism.
```
