# Unit 1 — Editorial — Team index + member detail

Attach: `devun/03`

Covers `/team` and `/team/[slug]` — two layouts, one prompt.

Reference: [BAMO](https://bamo.com/) puts principals and staff on the *homepage*, not buried in an
About page. At firm scale the people are the pitch — a client choosing between two studios is
choosing who will be in their house for four months.

Paste `00-identity-system.md` first, then this:

```
Design TWO pages: the TEAM INDEX and a TEAM MEMBER DETAIL page.

--- PAGE 1: TEAM INDEX ---

NAV — per Global Chrome, white background.

- Two-tone heading "OUR / STUDIO", small uppercase eyebrow above it
- One paragraph on the practice — size, philosophy, how the team works
- PRINCIPALS first: 2–3 people, given real size. Large portrait, name, role, one line on their
  focus. Offset outline frame on each portrait
- THE WIDER TEAM below: 12–18 people in a tighter grid. Portrait, name, role. Smaller, but not
  thumbnail-small — these are people, not logos
- Design for a studio of 20. Show how the grid handles that without becoming a wall of faces
- A closing block: "We're hiring" with a link to careers

--- PAGE 2: TEAM MEMBER DETAIL ---

- Large portrait, offset outline frame
- Name, role, years with the practice
- 2–3 paragraphs: background, training, what they focus on
- A ghost-numbered list of 3–4 projects they led, each linking to its case study
- Credentials line — qualifications, professional memberships
- Previous / next team member navigation
- FOOTER per Global Chrome

REQUIRED: at least 2 signature devices on each page.

ANTI-GOALS: same as the home page. Additionally — no social-media icon rows under each portrait,
no hover-flip cards, no "fun fact" or favourite-coffee copy. This is a professional practice, not
a startup about-page.
```
