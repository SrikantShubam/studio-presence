# Unit 1 — Editorial — News & press index + article

Attach: none required

Covers `/news` and `/news/[slug]` — two layouts, one prompt.

Reference: [BAMO](https://bamo.com/) carries *News & Press* at nav level, not in a footer. For a
firm bidding on serious work, third-party coverage is the credibility that a self-written About
page can't buy.

Paste `00-identity-system.md` first, then this:

```
Design TWO pages: the NEWS & PRESS INDEX and a NEWS ARTICLE page.

--- PAGE 1: NEWS & PRESS INDEX ---

NAV — per Global Chrome, white background.

Two-tone heading "NEWS / & PRESS". Two distinct content types on this page, visually separated:

1. PRESS COVERAGE — external. Each entry:
   - Publication name, prominent (Architectural Digest, Elle Decor India, The Hindu)
   - Article headline
   - Date
   - A pull-quote from the piece if there is one
   - "Read on [publication] ↗" — an outbound link with an external-link arrow
   - Publication logo if supplied, greyscale
   These do NOT open an internal article page. They leave the site. Make that legible at a glance.

2. STUDIO NEWS — internal. Each entry:
   - Headline, date, a 2-line summary, one image
   - Links to an internal article page
   Typical items: an award, a project completion, a new office, a hire.

Present them as one reverse-chronological stream with a clear visual distinction between the two
kinds, or as two stacked sections — your call, but the difference between "someone wrote about us"
and "we announced something" must be immediately obvious. The first is worth far more.

Show 12 entries. Include a year filter as text links: "2026 · 2025 · 2024".

--- PAGE 2: NEWS ARTICLE (internal only) ---

- Two-tone headline, date, a category label ("Award" / "Project" / "Studio")
- Lead image with offset outline frame
- 400–600 words of body copy with proper typographic hierarchy
- A pull-quote treatment
- Related project card if the item is about one
- Previous / next article
- FOOTER per Global Chrome

REQUIRED: at least 2 signature devices on each page.

ANTI-GOALS: same as the home page. Additionally — no social share buttons, no comment section, no
"related posts you may like" algorithmic block, no reading-time estimate. And press entries must
never look like internal articles; a visitor should never click expecting to stay on the site and
find themselves leaving, or vice versa.
```
