# Unit 1 — Editorial — Estimate calculator

Attach: none required

**Optional at every tier.** A light engagement gimmick — three inputs, a ballpark range, a CTA.
Some clients will want it, some won't. `estimate: false` turns it off.

Don't over-build it. It gives a visitor something to do and a reason to message; it isn't the
centrepiece of the site. Agency-tier firms often prefer to leave pricing to a conversation — that's
a per-client call, not a rule either way.

Paste `00-identity-system.md` first, then this:

```
Design the ESTIMATE CALCULATOR page. Keep it simple — a light interactive tool, not a major feature.

NAV — per Global Chrome, white background.

Two-tone heading "CALCULATE / THE ESTIMATE", one line beneath: "A rough idea of what your project
might cost."

THREE INPUTS, nothing more:
1. Carpet area — a slider, 500–4000 sq ft
2. Home type — 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 BHK, selectable
3. Finish level — Essential / Premium / Luxe, selectable

RESULT: a price RANGE, updating live as inputs change. No submit button.
   "₹6.5 – 8.2 lakh"
   Labelled beneath: "Indicative only. Final quote depends on scope and materials."

Below the result, a gold CTA: "Get an exact quote on WhatsApp".

Show the pre-interaction state — sensible defaults already chosen, a result already visible, so
nobody lands on an empty screen.

FOOTER — per Global Chrome.

Keep the whole thing on one screen where possible. Ghost numerals beside the three inputs if it
suits the layout, but don't force it.

ANTI-GOALS: same as the home page. Additionally — never show a figure without the range and the
"indicative" label. No lead-capture gate before the result, no email-required step, no multi-step
wizard, no progress bar. If someone has to give up their email to see a ballpark number, the gimmick
stops being charming and becomes a form.
```
