# Unit 1 — Editorial — Office location page

Attach: `devun/04`

Covers `/locations/[office]`.

Reference: [BAMO](https://bamo.com/) runs five offices — San Francisco, Providence, Miami, NYC,
Barcelona — with the list in the footer. [Tredi](https://www.trediinteriors.com/modern-interior-design-palm-springs/)
runs two markets as full landing pages.

**Distinct from `/areas/[locality]`.** An area page is an SEO landing page for a neighbourhood the
firm *works in*. This is a page for an office the firm *has* — a real address, real staff, real
opening hours. Different intent, different content, no doorway-page risk.

Paste `00-identity-system.md` first, then this:

```
Design the OFFICE LOCATION page for one office: "Patna Studio".

NAV — per Global Chrome, white background.

STRUCTURE:
- Two-tone heading "PATNA / STUDIO", eyebrow above: "OUR OFFICES / IN INDIA"
- A hero photograph of the actual studio space, offset outline frame
- CONTACT BLOCK, given real prominence: full address, phone, WhatsApp, email, opening hours.
  This is the page's job — someone came here to find or contact this office
- An embedded map
- "WHO'S HERE" — 3–4 team members based at this office, portraits and roles, linking to their
  team pages
- PROJECTS FROM THIS STUDIO — 4–6 project cards completed by this office
- A short paragraph on the office itself: when it opened, what work it focuses on, the market
  it serves
- "OUR OTHER STUDIOS" — a row of plain text links to the firm's other offices
- Gold CTA: "Visit us — book an appointment on WhatsApp"
- FOOTER per Global Chrome

REQUIRED: at least 2 signature devices.

ANTI-GOALS: same as the home page. Additionally — this must not read as a generic contact page with
a city name inserted. The studio photograph, the people, and the projects all have to be specific to
this office. If it could become the "Lucknow Studio" page by find-and-replace, it shouldn't ship —
same standard as the area pages, for the same reason.
```
