## Tier positioning

### Presence

Presence is the standard entry website. It uses an approved Studio Presence template and gives a
studio the core pages, contact paths, hosting, and a controlled editor. It does not include the
blog, Google Reviews, multiple locations, or custom design work.

The blog can be enabled later for ₹3,999 including GST when the shared editorial workflow is ready.
That add-on includes the same 100-published-post limit as Presence Plus. It does not include
writing, migration, or unlimited upload work.

### Presence Plus

Presence Plus is the complete standard package. It still uses an approved Studio Presence template,
but adds the content and proof a more established studio needs: more projects, locations,
before-and-after work, Google Reviews, service detail pages, a visual editor, and the journal.

The journal limit is 100 published posts. There is no monthly publishing quota. The client can
publish those posts through the editor; Studio Presence does not provide unlimited writing or
upload labour.

### Growth

Growth is not a larger standard template. It is a custom engagement for a studio that needs work
outside the three approved template systems.

Growth from ₹53,999 includes three pre-launch revision rounds. Growth Custom from ₹64,999 includes
five. The final quote names the custom pages, sections, branding, icon design, content structures,
editor changes, migration work, and integrations. More revisions or new scope cost more.

Do not sell Growth as a vague package of richer presentation. Sell it as custom design and
implementation work with a written scope.

## Reporting rule

Every tier uses the same Umami-backed analytics source. The tier difference is the reporting view:

- Presence: current-period visitors, all-time total, and top pages.
- Presence Plus: trends, comparisons, and more retained history.
- Growth: deeper date filters, exports, and implemented campaign views when available.

Do not claim WhatsApp conversation tracking for a normal WhatsApp link.

## Scope rule

Portfolio counts, locations, service pages, and blog limits are commercial setup allowances. They do
not permanently prevent a client from adding content later. New pages, custom layouts, large
migrations, and new integrations require a written quote.

Testimonials and client-supplied reviews have no quantity cap.

## Revision and support rule

Revision rounds happen before launch. Launch support begins after the approved site goes live.
Support covers defects in the delivered scope and small supplied-content corrections. It does not
include new pages, redesigns, new content types, writing, or new integrations.
