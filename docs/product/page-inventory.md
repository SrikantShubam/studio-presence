# Page inventory and tier composition

> **Commercial reference.** `docs/product/SPEC.md` is the spec of record. This file describes the
> current commercial scope and replaces older notes that treated Growth as a fixed page bundle.

Prices include 18% GST.

## Terminology

| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| Template | One complete standard Studio Presence site design |
| Page layout | A separately addressable page type with its own URL and content structure |
| Section | A block inside a page |
| Project record | A portfolio item that may produce a project detail URL |
| Setup allowance | The amount of initial content entry included in the project price |

Testimonials and client-supplied reviews are not capped. Project, location, service-page, and journal
limits describe initial setup or the agreed content model. They are not permanent technical locks.

## Current commercial ladder

| Tier | Price including GST | Template model | Revision rounds |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Presence | ₹17,999 | Approved standard template | 2 |
| Presence Plus | ₹29,999 | Approved standard template with expanded content | 3 |

Presence and Presence Plus use the approved template systems. Growth is not a standard third tier
with a fixed collection of layouts.

## Presence

Presence is the standard entry website for one studio and one primary location.

### Initial content scope

- Homepage
- About section
- Services section
- Portfolio section
- Contact section
- Up to 3 initial projects
- 1 primary location
- Testimonials with no quantity cap
- WhatsApp and call actions
- Simple website enquiry form
- Thank-you page with the form
- Controlled visual editor
- One month of launch support with 3 small request batches

### Standard layouts

The standard build includes the homepage, project-detail layout for supplied projects, generated
legal pages, utility pages, system assets, and the universal panel. The exact routes belong in the
client contract.

### Optional journal

Presence does not include the journal by default. The journal can be enabled for ₹3,999 including
GST when the shared editorial workflow exists. The add-on includes the standard journal layout, up to
3 supplied posts uploaded, and a 100-published-post limit. Writing, migration, and custom layouts are
separate work.

## Presence Plus

Presence Plus is the fullest standard package. It is still based on an approved Studio Presence
template, but it gives an established studio more proof and more content structure.

### Initial content scope

- Everything in Presence
- Up to 8 initial projects
- Up to 3 named locations
- Before-and-after section
- Google Reviews block
- Small named set of service detail pages
- Visual editor for approved fields and journal posts
- Blog/journal module
- Up to 100 published posts
- One digital-card setup when the feature is ready
- Umami trends and comparison views
- One month of launch support with 4 small request batches

### Page principle

Service and location pages count only when they contain distinct information. A copied page with a
changed locality is not a useful content page and should not be sold as local SEO.

## Growth and Growth Custom

Growth is custom work outside the approved standard templates.

### Growth from ₹53,999

The quote may include:

- a custom visual direction;
- bespoke page layouts;
- custom sections or components;
- custom project or case-study presentation;
- new content types;
- custom editor fields or workflows;
- branding and custom icon design;
- content migration;
- custom forms or integrations; and
- 3 pre-launch revision rounds.

The quote must name the pages, layouts, content structures, assets, integrations, and revision
rounds. "Custom website" is not a scope.

### Growth Custom from ₹64,999

Use this level when the project needs deeper custom work, such as:

- multiple custom page families;
- a custom content model;
- a custom editor workflow;
- large migration work;
- multiple integrations;
- additional branding and icon work; or
- 5 pre-launch revision rounds.

Work above this scope receives a separate quote.

Journal access may be included when needed. The current commercial allowance is up to 1,000
published posts. This is a post-record limit, not unlimited media storage.

## Reporting

Every tier uses the same Umami-backed analytics source.

| Tier | Reporting view |
|---|---|
| Presence | Current period visitors, all-time total, top pages |
| Presence Plus | Trends, comparisons, and more retained history |
| Growth | Deeper date filters, exports, and implemented campaign views when available |

Do not describe these as separate analytics products. Do not claim WhatsApp conversation tracking for
a normal WhatsApp link.

## Support and revisions

Revision rounds happen before launch. Launch support begins after the approved site goes live.

| Tier | Pre-launch rounds | Launch support |
|---|---:|---|
| Presence | 2 | 30 days, 3 small request batches |
| Presence Plus | 3 | 30 days, 4 small request batches |
| Growth | 3 | 30 days, 6 small request batches |
| Growth Custom | 5 | 30 days, 6 small request batches |

Launch support covers delivered-scope defects and small supplied-content corrections. It does not
include new pages, redesigns, new content types, writing, migration, or new integrations.

## Deliberately separate from standard scope

The following are never implied by a tier:

- CRM implementation;
- WhatsApp Business conversation sync;
- lead qualification or sales follow-up;
- custom dashboard workflows;
- multilingual content without a named language scope;
- paid advertising;
- ranking guarantees;
- photography, video production, or asset licensing; and
- unlimited design revisions.

Each requires a written scope and price.
