# Studio Intake Form — used by intern after a lead says "yes, show me"

Purpose: minimum data needed to spin a real (not fake-LLM-guessed) demo. Takes the owner ~3 minutes on WhatsApp or a form link.

1. **Studio name** (exact, as they'd want it displayed)
2. **Area / locality** (for local SEO + map pin)
3. **What you offer** (e.g. "Hatha & power yoga, beginner to advanced")
4. **Class timings** (rough is fine — "Mon-Sat, 6-8am and 6-8pm")
5. **3 photos** (studio space, a class in session, owner/instructor — phone photos are fine)
6. **Contact number for bookings** (where the WhatsApp CTA should route)
7. **Instagram handle** (for the embed) — optional but recommended
8. **Anything you want visitors to see first?** (free-text, one line — often reveals their actual selling point)

## Rules for the intern
- Do NOT build a demo without at least items 1–4 and one photo. A demo built on guessed/wrong facts (wrong timings, wrong area) reads as sloppy, not impressive — kills the pitch instead of making it.
- If the studio won't send photos or basic info within 48 hours after agreeing, mark as stalled — don't chase indefinitely, move to next lead.
- Log every field + date received in the lead tracker (master CSV), not just "sent info."
