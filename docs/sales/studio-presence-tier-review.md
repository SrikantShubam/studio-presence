## The commercial decision

Prices include 18% GST:

- Presence: ₹17,999
- Presence Plus: ₹29,999
- Growth: from ₹53,999
- Growth Custom: from ₹64,999

Presence and Presence Plus use the approved Studio Presence templates. Growth is custom design and
implementation work outside those standard templates.

## Growth scope

Growth is for a customer who asks for work outside the three approved templates. A Growth quote may
include:

- custom visual direction;
- bespoke page layouts;
- new sections and components;
- custom project or case-study presentation;
- new content types;
- custom editor fields or workflows;
- branding and custom icon design;
- content migration;
- custom forms or integrations; and
- additional design and approval rounds.

The quote must list the pages, custom structures, assets, revision rounds, and integrations by name.
The phrase "custom website" by itself is not a scope.

Growth from ₹53,999 includes three pre-launch revision rounds. Growth Custom from ₹64,999 includes
five. New revisions or new requirements after approval are change orders.

## Support decision

All three tiers include one month of launch support. Launch support is not a monthly maintenance
subscription. It covers defects in the delivered scope and small supplied-content corrections.

The included request batches are:

- Presence: 3 batches;
- Presence Plus: 4 batches; and
- Growth: 6 batches.

A batch groups related small edits into one request. New pages, new layouts, new content types,
writing, migration, integrations, and redesigns are separate work.

## Reporting decision

Umami is the analytics source for every tier. Do not describe the packages as using different
analytics systems.

- Presence shows a simple visitor and top-page summary.
- Presence Plus adds trends and comparisons.
- Growth adds deeper filters and exports when the reporting implementation supports them.

WhatsApp clicks are not WhatsApp conversations. Conversation sync remains a separate future
integration.

## Implementation gate

Before publishing a package promise, confirm:

- the visual editor can safely edit the fields named in that tier;
- the blog workflow supports drafts, preview, publishing, and the stated post limit;
- the form writes to the tenant-scoped enquiry pipeline;
- Umami can produce the stated reporting view; and
- custom Growth scope is recorded before work starts.
