## Questions people actually ask

**"Do I own the website?"**
You own all your content — text, photos, project details. We own the underlying template and code,
which is standard. Your domain is registered in your name.

**"What if I want to leave?"**
We package up your site and content for a flat fee and hand it over. Your domain is already yours,
so it keeps working.

**"What if you disappear?"**
Your domain is in your name, so it stays yours regardless. And we're a registered firm with a GST
number and a contract — the whole reason we set it up that way is so you don't have to take our word
for it.

**"Two days really?"**
Once you send us your photos and details, yes. Most of the wait is usually waiting for photos.

**"I already have a website."**
Then you probably don't need us. If it's not showing up on Google or not getting you enquiries,
that's a different conversation — happy to have it.
