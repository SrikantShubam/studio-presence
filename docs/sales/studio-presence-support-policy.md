# Studio Presence support and renewal policy

Status: proposed customer policy.

This policy applies to Presence, Presence Plus, and Growth. It separates the one-month launch
support included with setup from later renewal care and separately quoted work.

## One month of launch support

Every setup includes 30 days of launch support after the approved website goes live.

### Included

- fixes for defects in the delivered scope;
- broken-link, form, and contact-path fixes caused by our implementation;
- small text corrections;
- supplied image replacements;
- phone, email, address, and opening-hours corrections;
- small project or service updates; and
- guidance on using the editor.

### Request limits

| Tier | Included during the 30-day window |
|---|---|
| Presence | 3 small request batches |
| Presence Plus | 4 small request batches |
| Growth | 6 small request batches |

A batch groups related edits into one request. For example, replacing three supplied images and
correcting a phone number is one batch.

Bugs in the delivered scope do not consume a content batch.

### Excluded from launch support

- new pages;
- new sections or layouts;
- new content types;
- redesigns;
- blog writing or large post uploads;
- new locations outside the agreed scope;
- custom icon or brand work not in the quote;
- new integrations;
- SEO campaigns or ranking work; and
- WhatsApp conversation handling.

Those requests receive a separate quote.

## Revisions before launch

Pre-launch revision rounds are separate from launch support:

| Tier | Included rounds |
|---|---:|
| Presence | 2 |
| Presence Plus | 3 |
| Growth from ₹53,999 | 3 |
| Growth Custom from ₹64,999 | 5 |

An extra revision round is quoted before work begins. A revision round is one grouped review of the
current approved scope. A new design direction or new requirement is not a revision.

## Renewal

Renewal keeps the agreed site online. It is not a subscription for new product features.

| Tier | Yearly renewal | Included care | Response target |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Presence | ₹2,500, plus domain cost when applicable | Hosting, SSL, backups, monitoring, maintenance, and 2 small batches per year | Within 5 business days |
| Presence Plus | ₹3,500, plus domain cost when applicable | Everything in Presence, plus 4 small batches per year | Within 4 business days |
| Growth | ₹5,000, plus domain cost when applicable | Everything in Plus, plus 8 small batches per year and one quarterly analytics review | Within 3 business days |

Renewal prices should be reviewed against actual platform and domain costs before the next billing
cycle.

## Care plans

Monthly care is optional. It does not replace yearly hosting renewal.

| Tier | Monthly price | Included work |
|---|---:|---|
| Presence care | ₹499 | Up to 2 small batches per month |
| Plus care | ₹999 | Up to 4 small batches per month |
| Growth care | ₹1,999 | Up to 8 small batches and one monthly analytics review |

Use a three-month minimum for monthly care. Do not promise unlimited revisions, same-day work,
marketing, lead qualification, or WhatsApp conversation handling.

## Recommended public wording

> Every website includes one month of launch support for small content corrections and fixes within
> the agreed scope. After that, renewal keeps the site online and includes a small number of content
> updates. New pages, redesigns, branding, and integrations are quoted separately.
