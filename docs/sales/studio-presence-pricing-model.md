# Studio Presence pricing model

Status: internal working model. Do not publish these calculations as a guarantee.

This model uses the current customer-facing prices, 18% GST, a working 0.4% payment rate, and a
25% intern commission. The intern's ₹5,000 monthly base is tracked separately and is covered by the
existing reserve. It is not deducted again from each sale.

| Tier | Customer price including GST | Service value before GST | GST component |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Presence | ₹17,999 | ₹15,253.39 | ₹2,745.61 |
| Presence Plus | ₹29,999 | ₹25,422.88 | ₹4,576.12 |
| Growth | From ₹53,999 | From ₹45,761.86 | From ₹8,237.14 |
| Growth Custom | From ₹64,999 | From ₹55,083.90 | From ₹9,915.10 |

GST collected from the customer is not profit. Eligible input tax credit may change the net tax
position, but the accountant must confirm that treatment.

## Working inputs

| Input | Working value | Replace with |
|---|---:|---|
| GST | 18% | Accountant-confirmed rate |
| Payment rate | 0.40% | Actual provider settlement statement |
| GST on payment fee | 18% | Actual provider invoice |
| Intern commission | 25% of service value before GST | Written commission agreement |
| Intern base | ₹5,000 per month | Covered separately by the existing reserve |
| Staff cost | ₹0 currently | Actual staff cost when staff exists |
| Per-sale tooling and operating cost | ₹600–₹1,500 | Actual invoices and usage |

The payment provider may calculate its fee on the gross customer collection or on another settlement
base. Confirm the base before finalising the model.

## Growth pricing

Growth is custom work, not a larger fixed template package.

| Price point | Minimum scope decision |
|---|---|
| ₹53,999 including GST | Custom design direction, named custom pages or sections, and 3 revision rounds |
| ₹64,999 including GST | Deeper custom design, multiple custom page families, migration or custom editor work, and 5 revision rounds |
| Above ₹64,999 | Quote after listing the custom requirements, assets, integrations, and delivery time |

The quote must name the revision rounds. A new requirement after approval is a change order, not a
free revision.

## Blog add-on

The Presence blog add-on is ₹3,999 including GST when the shared editorial workflow already exists.
It includes the standard journal layout, up to three supplied posts uploaded, and a 100-published-post
limit. Writing, migration, large uploads, custom layouts, and image sourcing are separate work.

## Pricing decision

Keep the prices visible and simple:

- Presence: ₹17,999 including GST;
- Presence Plus: ₹29,999 including GST;
- Growth: from ₹53,999 including GST; and
- Growth Custom: from ₹64,999 including GST.

Do not publish a vague ₹53,999–₹64,999 range without explaining the scope difference.

## Review triggers

Revisit this model after five to ten paid projects or when one of these occurs:

- delivery time regularly exceeds the scope allowance;
- the editor or journal adds measurable support work;
- the actual payment settlement differs from the 0.4% assumption;
- staff are hired;
- Growth custom work repeatedly exceeds the 3-round or 5-round allowance; or
- customers accept the price without negotiation and the delivery process is stable.
