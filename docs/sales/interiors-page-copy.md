## 9. FAQ *(new — it's in the nav but doesn't exist)*

- **Why should I trust you with my studio's website?**
  Fair question — don't take our word for it. We're a registered firm, you get a written contract
  and a proper invoice, and you see the site working before you pay anything.
- **Do I own the website?**
  You own all your content — text, photos, project details. Your domain is registered in your name.
  We own the underlying template and code, which is standard for template-based work and why the
  price is what it is.
- **What if I want to leave later?**
  We package your site and content and hand it over for a flat fee. Your domain is already yours,
  so it keeps working.
- **Two days, really?**
  Once you send photos and details, yes. Most of the wait is usually waiting for photos.
- **I already have a website.**
  Then you may not need us. If it isn't showing up on Google or bringing enquiries, that's a
  different conversation — happy to have it.
- **What if I don't have good photos?**
  Phone photos are fine. We clean up lighting and crops as part of the build, and we'll send you a
  one-page guide on shooting rooms.
