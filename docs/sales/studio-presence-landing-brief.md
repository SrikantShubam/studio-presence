# Studio Presence landing-page brief

Status: customer-facing handoff for the landing-page build.

Use this file as the copy source. Do not use internal product history or unfinished feature notes as
public copy.

## The product

Studio Presence builds websites for Indian interior-design studios.

The site gives a studio one place to show its work, explain its services, list its locations, and
give prospects a clear way to enquire.

The main contact path is WhatsApp. The site can measure visits and website enquiries. It cannot read
or measure a normal WhatsApp conversation.

## The pitch

### Hero

**Your studio has the work. Give it a proper place to be found.**

Studio Presence builds a complete website for interior studios. Show your projects, services,
locations, and contact options in one place.

**From ₹17,999 including GST. One-time setup.**

Primary CTA: Book a demo

Secondary CTA: Message us on WhatsApp

## The problem

Good work gets lost when a prospect finds only an Instagram page or a directory listing.

Before contacting a studio, a prospect wants to see:

- what the studio does;
- what it has built;
- where it works; and
- how to make contact.

## What the site includes

- portfolio;
- services;
- about and process;
- testimonials;
- location and contact details;
- WhatsApp and call actions;
- enquiry path;
- mobile-first layout; and
- Umami-backed visitor reporting.

Do not promise a lead count, ranking, revenue, return on investment, or WhatsApp conversation
tracking.

## Why Studio Presence

Anyone can assemble a page. The work is making the studio clear to a serious prospect and keeping
the site usable after launch.

The offer should promise:

- a written list of included pages and project setup;
- a clear review process;
- hosting and domain handling explained before launch;
- a Studio Presence editor for supported content; and
- one month of launch support with stated limits.

Use claims about GST registration, legal status, clients, performance scores, and testimonials only
after verification.

## How it works

1. The studio shares its name, locations, services, photos, projects, and contact details.
2. We prepare the site and show the first version.
3. The studio reviews the agreed scope.
4. We make the included revision rounds and launch on the agreed domain.
5. The studio uses the editor, or asks for separately quoted changes.
